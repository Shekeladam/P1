The Club

The pride that wouldn't stay quiet

FC Lions started in 2019 as a supporters' collective who pooled matchday money to stop a non-league ground from being sold off for flats — and ended up owning the club outright. Six years, three promotions, and one very literal stadium name later, Roar Stadium is exactly what it sounds like on a Saturday afternoon.
